# Studio screens

The old page shows videos as tiles with covers and a button to start the next reel.
The studio screens open at `/new`.
Each reel card shows its status, and the reel's top bar menu lets you rename it.
The reel list shows reels and uploaded takes five to a page.
You can upload takes and resume an interrupted upload from that list.

1. **«Записи».** Tick the uploaded takes to add them to your reel at once.
   Press a word to play the recording from that point.
1. **«План».** Save the task with its apps and files, then approve the plan.
   The chat streams its reply on every step and has an «Остановить» button.
1. **«Монтаж».** The studio lists the parts with their takes, pauses, words, texts, and inserts.
   Use «Вопросы» to check doubtful words, joins, inserts, and on-screen wording.
   Keep, rewrite, or remove each title and key thought before you build.
   Use «Отменить» to undo a change.
1. **«Оформление».** Play the reel and switch between the three «Шум» choices to hear the room noise change.
   The studio uses your choice when it builds the reel and leaves the original recording alone.

Open «Правила» on the step a rule applies to.
Change a rule in chat and pin it to keep it for the next reel.
See [Takes and blocks](timeline.md) for the rules and how they work.
