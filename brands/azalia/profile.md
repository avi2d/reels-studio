# Profile azalia

The style repeats the site azalialaw.com.

## Colours

| Name | Value | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| roast | `#28080f` | dark background of a plate |
| wine | `#4d0c12` | plate under a key thought |
| ruby | `#6d343c` | thin lines and secondary plates |
| rose | `#d8a7b1` | one accented word, never a whole line |
| milk | `#f6f3e4` | every text |

Milk text goes on a roast or wine plate, and rose text never goes on wine.

## Fonts

- Playfair Display for a title and a key thought.
- Noto Serif Display Condensed for captions.

## Templates

| Template | In the plan | What it is | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Title | `титул` | The name of the video at the start | 2 lines, 22 characters each |
| Key thought | `тезис` | A plate with one thought | 3 lines, 26 characters each |
| Captions | `субтитры` | The spoken words over the lower part | 2 lines, 32 characters each |

The limits are a working estimate until the templates are measured with the real fonts.
