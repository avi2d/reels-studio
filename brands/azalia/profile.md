# Profile azalia

The style repeats Azalia's reference reels: light serif words straight over the video, with one pale pink italic line for the point.

## Colours

| Name | Value | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| white | `#fefcfd` | subtitles and the upright lines of a key thought |
| blush | `#f1c3ce` | the italic accent line of a key thought |

Text goes over the video without a plate.

## Fonts

The fonts are in [fonts/](fonts/) under the SIL Open Font License.

| Font | Size at 1080 by 1920 | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Noto Serif Display | 78 | subtitles |
| Noto Serif Display Condensed | 100 | the upright lines of a key thought |
| Reels Accent, Playfair Display Italic with lining digits | 230 | the accent line of a key thought |

The reference reels use fonts the studio could not name, and these are the nearest licensed ones.

## Templates

| Template | In the plan | What it is | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Title | `титул` | A key thought over the first phrase that names the video | as a key thought |
| Key thought | `тезис` | Upright lines with one accent line, in the upper left of the box | 3 lines; an upright line 20 characters, the accent line 8 |
| Subtitles | `субтитры` | The spoken words, one at a time, low in the centre | one word; long words are drawn smaller to fit |

The key-thought limits are the average width of Russian text in these fonts across the box.
A line of wide letters such as «ж», «ш» or «ю» fits fewer.
The studio measures every text before the render.
It stops the render when a key thought leaves the box, and draws a long subtitle word smaller.
