# Vision

A lawyer turns a recorded video into a finished Instagram reel by writing in plain words, with no editor and no developer.
It serves one person who records the videos, and the owner who keeps the computer running.
It owns the path from an uploaded original to a reel that fits the Instagram player.

## The person reads plain Russian

- The page and the chat answer in plain Russian with no file name, command or model term, at the cost of detail a developer would want.

## The plan comes before any change

- The studio shows a plan and waits for a yes before it cuts or renders, at the cost of one more message in every video.

## The original is never touched

- The uploaded original is never changed, renamed or deleted, at the cost of disk space for every derived file.

## A text outside the Meta zone stops the render

- A text that leaves the zone the Instagram player keeps clear stops the render, with no softer warning, at the cost of a late failure.

## The video stays at home

- The studio answers only the home network and has no tunnel, public address or password, at the cost of no access from outside the home.
- Speech recognition runs on the home computer with an open model and sends nothing to a cloud service, at the cost of slower transcription.

## The home computer is left as it is

- The studio installs nothing system-wide and changes no Windows setting, at the cost of a firewall rule the owner applies by hand.

## Scope

- Publishing to Instagram is refused, and the person does it in the Instagram app.
- Storing videos in a cloud is refused, and the owner's own backup holds them.

A change aligns when it lets the person get a correct reel with fewer words and no technical knowledge.
A change should be resisted when it sends a video outside the home, alters an original or shows the person a technical term.
