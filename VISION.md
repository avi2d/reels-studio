# Vision

A lawyer turns a recorded video into a finished vertical video for each of Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok by writing in plain words, with no editor and no developer.
It serves one person who records the videos, and the owner who keeps the computer running.
It owns the path from an uploaded original to a separate video for each platform, each fitted to that platform's specifics, audience expectations, size and montage.

## The person reads plain Russian

- The page and the chat answer in plain Russian with no file name, command or model term.
  That costs the detail a developer would want.

## The plan comes before any change

- The studio shows one plan for the whole reel and waits for one yes before it cuts or renders, then asks again only about words on the screen, outside material and her face, at the cost of changes the person sees after they are made.

## The original is never touched

- The uploaded original is never changed, renamed or deleted, at the cost of disk space for every derived file.

## A text outside the clear zone stops the render

- A text that leaves the zone its player keeps clear stops the render, with no softer warning, at the cost of a late failure.
- Each player's clear zone comes from numbers it publishes or from measuring it, at the cost of measuring again when a player changes.

## A program comes first, and the chat model may see video

- The chat model runs in a cloud and may read the words of a video, its frames and the files the person attaches, at the cost of her words and pictures leaving the home.
- Where a program on the home computer gives a good result, the studio uses the program and does not ask the model, at the cost of more code to write.

## The studio answers at home and on the owner's Mac

- The studio answers the home network and the owner's Mac through the `local-apps` gateway.
  The gateway listens only on that Mac's loopback address.
  The studio has no public address or password, at the cost of no public access.
- Speech recognition runs on the home computer with an open model and sends nothing to a cloud service, at the cost of slower transcription.

## The home computer is left as it is

- The studio installs nothing system-wide and changes no Windows setting, at the cost of a firewall rule the owner applies by hand.

## Scope

- Publishing is refused, and the person does it in each app.
- Storing videos in a cloud is refused, and the owner's own backup holds them.

A change aligns when it lets the person get a correct video with fewer words and no technical knowledge.
A change should be resisted when it sends a video to a cloud service other than the chat model, alters an original or shows the person a technical term.
